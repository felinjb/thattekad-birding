Early Morning Chorus

3-4 Hours   |   Starts at 6:00 AM

This 3-4 hour trek starts right at dawn. It is the absolute best time for spotting active songbirds, hornbills, and raptors. The forest canopy comes alive during these hours, offering unparalleled bird activity before the heat of the day sets in.

Expected Itinerary:

  • 06:00 AM: Meet your expert guide at the sanctuary entrance.
  • 06:15 AM: Enter the forest and track the active morning canopy.
  • 08:30 AM: Photography and observation break near the riverbed.
  • 09:30 AM: Tour concludes with a review of species spotted.

Night Birding Tour

2-3 Hours   |   Starts at 7:00 PM

Using specialized low-light techniques, we safely track nocturnal life without disturbing them. This is your best opportunity to see the legendary Ceylon Frogmouth, Sri Lanka Bay Owl, and various nightjars.

Expected Itinerary:

  • 06:30 PM: Gather for safety briefing and flashlight protocols.
  • 06:45 PM: Silent walk begins into known nightjars territories.
  • 07:00 PM: Tracking owl calls and nightjars along the forest edge.
  • 08:30 PM - 09:00 PM: Safe return to the starting point.

Thattekad Intensive Multiday

3 Nights / 4 Days   |   Accommodation & Meals Included

A deep, multi-day immersion into the Salim Ali Bird Sanctuary. This package maximizes your chances of spotting and photographing both diurnal and nocturnal target species at a relaxed pace.

Key Targets: Sri Lanka Frogmouth, Malabar Trogon, Black Baza, Sri Lanka Bay Owl, Mottled Wood Owl.

Expected Itinerary:

  • Day 1: Check-in & afternoon walk tracking woodpeckers and bulbuls.
  • Day 2: Deep forest trek (AM) for mid-canopy endemics. Specialized night walk (PM) for owls/frogmouths.
  • Day 3: Golden hour hide photography (AM) & custom target tracking (PM).
  • Day 4: Early Morning Chorus walk, breakfast & departure.

Thattekad & Munnar Expedition

4 Nights / 5 Days   |   Lowland & High-Altitude

The ultimate Western Ghats birding experience. Transition from the dense, humid lowland forests of Thattekad to the crisp, high-altitude shola-grassland ecosystems of Munnar to encounter a completely distinct set of endemic species.

Key Targets: Kerala Laughingthrush, Nilgiri Pipit, Black-and-orange Flycatcher, White-bellied Blue Robin, Indian Pitta, Malabar Trogon.

Expected Itinerary:

  • Day 1: Arrival in Thattekad, check-in, and afternoon edge habitat birding.
  • Day 2: Deep forest trek for Thattekad endemics (AM) & Night birding for frogmouths (PM).
  • Day 3: Final Thattekad walk, scenic drive to Munnar, and afternoon shola birding.
  • Day 4: Full-day exploration of Munnar's high elevation hotspots (e.g., Eravikulam).
  • Day 5: Early morning high-altitude targets in Munnar, breakfast, and departure.

Booking, Payment & Cancellation

Every trip is quoted for your group and confirmed on receipt of full advance payment. Because we reserve guides and accommodation well in advance, especially in peak season, confirmed bookings cannot be cancelled. Please check your dates and details carefully before you pay.

Liability Waiver & Assumption of Risk

Wildlife is wild and unpredictable. We do not guarantee the sighting of any specific species. Guests accept the inherent physical risks of jungle trekking (uneven terrain, weather hazards, wildlife/leech encounters) and are responsible for their own medical fitness and emergency medications.

We collect your details strictly for tour coordination and invoicing. We do not sell your data. All website content and photography is the intellectual property of Thattekad Birding. By booking, you agree to strict ethical wildlife conduct guidelines.

Cookies & third parties. Only with your consent, we use Google Analytics and Google Ads (Google) and, on some pages, Plerdy to measure visits and our advertising. Nothing loads until you accept, and you can change your choice at any time using “Cookie settings” in the footer. Gear & Attire

Wear dull-colored, breathable clothing and closed-toe trekking shoes. Leech socks are highly recommended post-monsoon. Bring your own binoculars and camera gear. No playback calls or flash photography on nocturnal species.
