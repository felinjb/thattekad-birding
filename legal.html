Legal & Policies

Our commitments, operational terms, and guest responsibilities.

Privacy Policy Terms & Conditions Liability Waiver & Risk Booking & Cancellation

1. Privacy Policy

Welcome to Thattekad Birding. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data.

Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when expressing an interest in obtaining information about our tours, booking a trip, or contacting us. This includes your Name, Email Address, Phone/WhatsApp Number, and Travel Preferences.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for operational purposes:

  • To facilitate tour bookings, scheduling, and itinerary planning.
  • To issue invoices and process payments.
  • To communicate with you via email or WhatsApp regarding your upcoming tour, meeting points, and preparation guides.

Data Storage and Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. Your data is stored securely using industry-standard cloud infrastructure. We only share necessary details (such as your name and target species) with our local assigned guides to ensure a successful tour.

2. Terms and Conditions

By accessing and using our website (thattekadbirding.com) and booking our services, you agree to be bound by the following terms.

Intellectual Property

All content on this website, including but not limited to wildlife photography, text, logos, graphics, and checklists, is the exclusive property of Thattekad Birding. You may not copy, reproduce, distribute, or commercially exploit any website content without our express written permission.

User Conduct

Our website and booking forms are intended for genuine inquiries. Malicious use, spamming, or attempting to compromise the website's infrastructure is strictly prohibited.

Ethical Wildlife Conduct

We enforce strict eco-tourism protocols. While on tour, guests must follow the guide's instructions. The use of playback calls to bait or stress birds, as well as flash photography on nocturnal species, is strictly prohibited. Guides hold the right to terminate a tour without refund if a guest engages in unethical behavior.

3. Liability Waiver & Assumption of Risk

Birding and herping in the Salim Ali Bird Sanctuary and surrounding Western Ghats involves traversing wild, natural environments. By booking a tour with Thattekad Birding, you acknowledge and accept the inherent risks involved.

Wildlife Unpredictability

Our guides are seasoned experts at tracking local endemics. However, wildlife is wild and unpredictable. We do not guarantee the sighting of any specific species (e.g., Ceylon Frogmouth, Malabar Trogon, Pittas, or Vipers). Failure to spot a target species does not constitute a failure of service and does not warrant a refund.

Physical & Medical Risks

Jungle trekking carries physical risks. You accept responsibility for hazards including, but not limited to: uneven terrain, slippery rocks, sudden monsoon weather changes, and encounters with local fauna (including leeches, insects, and snakes). Guests are expected to wear appropriate attire (closed-toe shoes, long trousers).

Medical Fitness

Guests are responsible for their own physical health and medical fitness. If you require emergency medication (e.g., EpiPens, asthma inhalers, insulin), you must carry it with you. Thattekad Birding and its guides are not liable for any injuries, allergic reactions, medical emergencies, or loss/damage to personal property (including expensive camera gear) sustained during the tour.

4. Booking, Payment & Cancellation Policy

To ensure fairness to our local guides and to secure accommodations during peak seasons, we maintain a strict booking framework.

Payment Terms

A tour is only confirmed upon the receipt of 100% full advance payment. Until payment is received, your requested dates remain open, and guide allocation is not guaranteed.

Strict No-Cancellation Policy

Because we reserve our guides' time and secure third-party accommodations well in advance—often turning away other guests during peak season—all confirmed bookings are strictly non-refundable and cannot be cancelled. Please verify your travel dates carefully prior to making payment.

Late Arrivals & No-Shows

Tours begin strictly at the agreed-upon time (especially critical for early morning dawn chorus walks). If you arrive late, the tour will still end at the originally scheduled time. Arriving late does not warrant a pro-rated refund or time extension. Failure to arrive at the meeting point constitutes a "No-Show," and the entire tour fee is forfeited.

Force Majeure & Sanctuary Closures

In the event that the Kerala Forest Department officially closes the sanctuary due to severe weather, flooding, landslides, or dangerous wildlife movement (e.g., wild elephants), we will attempt to reroute the tour to safe edge-habitats. If the tour must be entirely cancelled due to official government closure, we will offer a rescheduled date. Refunds in Force Majeure situations are solely at the discretion of Thattekad Birding management.
