Field Guide Spotlight

Malabar Trogon

Featuring a deep crimson breast, these stunning birds sit motionless in the mid-canopy waiting for flying insects.

Sri Lanka Frogmouth

An absolute Thattekad icon. Their camouflage makes them look completely identical to a cluster of dead brown leaves.

Grey-headed Fish Eagle

Frequently perched over the Periyar River, equipped with spiculated talons to securely grip slippery fish.

Malabar Giant Squirrel

Crashing through the upper canopy, these massive deep-maroon rodents can leap up to 20 feet between trees!
